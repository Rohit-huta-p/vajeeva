Coconut Laddu
Narikela modaka
Classical sources
🫙 Makes 3–4 laddoos 📅 Keeps 5–7 days
⚠ Use with caution
Diabetes · Overweight / Obesity · Lactose intolerance · Sedentary lifestyle
Ingredients
Grated coconut40–50 g
Milk30–40 ml
Ghee10–15 ml
Rock sugar powder15–20 g
Cardamom powder2–3 g
Dry ginger powder2–3 g
Method · 5 steps
  1. 1
    Milk & CoconutCook grated coconut in milk over low heat, stirring gently, until fully absorbed and well combined.
  2. 2
    Roast in gheeWarm the ghee in a heavy pan on low heat. Add coconut; roast, stirring, until light golden and fragrant.
  3. 3
    Sugar syrupPrepare a sugar syrup with rock sugar and a little water. Boil to a firm, binding consistency.
  4. 4
    CombineAdd roasted coconut to the syrup. Fold in cardamom and dry ginger powder; mix well off the heat.
  5. 5
    ShapeWhile the mixture is still warm, shape into small laddoos with your palms. Cool before serving.

About this text
A Sanskrit treatise on Ayurvedic culinary science. Classified as a Pakavidhi — a text on food preparation, cooking methods, and the properties of prepared dishes. One of the most detailed classical sources on sweet preparations.
This citation
Chapter 10, verse 54 — the 10th Utsava chapter covers sweet preparations (modaka). Verse 54 describes coconut-based sweets using milk, rock sugar, and warming spices as a nourishing preparation.
