Yield & storage
🫙 Makes 3–4 laddoos 📅 5–7 days
⚠ Use with caution
Diabetes  ·  Overweight  ·  Lactose intolerance  ·  Sedentary lifestyle
Ingredients
Grated coconut40–50 g
Milk30–40 ml
Ghee10–15 ml
Rock sugar powder15–20 g
Cardamom powder2–3 g
Dry ginger powder2–3 g
Method
  1. 1Cook grated coconut in milk until well combined and fully absorbed.
  2. 2Roast cooked coconut in ghee until fragrant and lightly golden.
  3. 3Prepare rock sugar syrup to a firm binding consistency.
  4. 4Add coconut to syrup; fold in cardamom and dry ginger powder.
  5. 5Shape into small laddoos while the mixture is still warm.
