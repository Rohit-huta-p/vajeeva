UX enhancements
①
Dark kitchen mode
Warm near-black (#1A1814) instead of bone — signals "you're cooking now", reduces screen glare in a dim kitchen
②
Circular countdown timer
Tap to start/pause/reset. SVG arc depletes as time runs. Visual at a glance — no need to read digits
③
Per-step ingredient chips
Scrollable row shows exactly what you need for this step — eliminates going back to the detail screen with oily hands
④
Step dots + heat badge
Amber pill tracks your position; tapping a past dot jumps back. Heat level (Low / Medium / Off) surfaced inline
⑤
Phase-tinted illustration
Illustration background shifts hue per cooking phase — green for milk, amber for roasting, neutral for shaping
⑥
Swipe + animated transition
Left/right swipe (touch + mouse drag) navigates steps. Text slides in from the direction of travel
⑦
Ambient progress line
2px amber rule at the very top of the screen — peripheral awareness of overall progress without cluttering the step view
⑧
Wake Lock API
navigator.wakeLock.request('screen') keeps display on while cooking. Re-acquired on tab visibility change
