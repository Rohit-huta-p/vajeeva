### N-07 · Meat Samosa *(Mamsa Samosa)*

**Source:** KK — Ksemakutūhalam 6/128–132 · ICMR
**Yield:** 2 medium samosa

| Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |
|-----------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Meat | 40–50 | — | ¼ cup | for filling |
| Wheat flour | 50 | — | ¼ cup | for dough |
| Hingu (asafoetida) | 3 | — | ½ tsp | — |
| Coriander powder | 5 | — | 1 tsp | — |
| Cumin powder | 5 | — | 1 tsp | — |
| Ghee | — | — | — | for dough & deep-frying |
| Rock salt | — | — | — | to taste |
| Lemon juice | — | — | — | to taste |
| Aromatics / spices | — | — | — | as preferred |

**Method**
Stuff preparation :
Wash the meat properly with water then boiled in water.
Take a boiled meat in mixing bowl and hingu, coriander powder mix it properly and crushed the meat finely.
Then take a pan add 2-3 tsp. of ghee heat it on medium flame then add hingu and mashed meat fry it well
Then add spices and flavours as per your taste and priority and add jeera powder, rock salt to taste then fried well then add lemon juice to taste and mix it well.
Dough Preparation :
Take a cup of wheat flour in a mixing bowl add rock salt to taste mix it well. then add 1-2tsp. of ghee mix well then gradually add water and knead well to make soft and smooth dough. Rest it for 15min.
Make equal sized ball from the dough. By Appling little quantity of ghee / oil to the ball makes a ball into thin and medium sized round roti.
After that prepared mamsa purana is filled in the prepared round roti and by closing the joints make into samosa shaped and then fried in ghee properly up to the samosa becomes golden colour and have it.
