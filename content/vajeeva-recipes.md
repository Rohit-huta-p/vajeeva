### S-01 · Barley Roti

**Source:** BK — Dhanyavishesha & Bhakshyavishesha, pp. 13, 51 · ICMR  
**Yield:** 2–3 medium roti

| Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |
|-----------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Barley flour | 80–100 | — | ½ cup | — |
| Water | — | — | — | as needed (warm preferred) |
| Rock salt | — | — | — | a pinch |
| Ghee | — | 4–6 | 1 tsp | — |

**Method**
1. Combine barley flour and rock salt. Gradually add warm water; knead into a soft, smooth dough. Cover and rest 15–20 min.
2. Divide into equal balls. Dust with flour and roll into thin, round rotis.
3. Cook on a medium-hot tawa. Apply ghee; cook until bubbles form, then flip. Apply ghee on second side; press gently with spatula until puffed and cooked through.
4. Serve hot with curry, dal, or chutney.

**Method**
1. Soak de-husked black gram 6–8 hr or overnight; grind to a smooth paste.

### S-06 · Baati *(Angarakarakati)*

**Source:** BP 12/33–34 · BK — Bhakshyavishesha, p. 51 · KK 10th Utsava · ICMR  
**Yield:** 2–3 medium baati

| Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |
|-----------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Wheat flour | 80–100 | — | ½ cup | — |
| Ghee | — | — | 3–4 tsp in | dough + extra for finishing |
| Water | — | — | — | as needed |
| Rock salt | — | — | — | a pinch |

**Method**
1. Mix flour, rock salt, and 3–4 tsp ghee. Add water gradually and knead into a firm dough. Rest 15–20 min.
2. Divide into medium balls. Cook using one of the following methods:
   - **Charcoal/ember:** Place balls on a wire rack over charcoal embers.
   - **Oven:** Bake at 180 °C for 25–30 min.
   - **Air fryer:** 15–20 min on oiled parchment.
   - **Pressure cooker:** Place on a rack over a salt/sand layer; low flame, 30–40 min, turning occasionally.
   - **Stovetop:** Thick-bottomed pan on low flame, turning occasionally; finish directly over low flame for colour.
3. Cool slightly. Dip in melted ghee or pour ghee generously over each. Serve with dal.

### S-37 · Stuffed Cottage Cheese Dumplings *(Dugdha Kupika)*

**Source:** BP 12/132–135 · ICMR  
**Yield:** 3–4 dumplings (1 serving)

| Stage | Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |
|-------|-----------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Chena | Cow's milk | — | 500 | — | — |
|  | Lemon juice | — | 10 | — | to curdle |
| Dough | Rice flour | 10 | — | 2 tsp | — |
| Filling | Rock sugar | 20 | — | — | — |
|  | Edible camphor | — | — | — | a pinch |
| Syrup | Sugar | 20 | — | — | + 40 ml water |
| Frying | Ghee | 150 | — | — | — |

**Method**
1. **Make chena:** Boil milk; add lemon juice gradually while stirring until milk curdles. Strain through muslin, wash with clean water, hang 20–30 min to drain.
2. **Dough:** Add rice flour to chena; knead 8–10 min until smooth and crack-free.
3. **Shape:** Divide into 3–4 balls (25–30 g each). Roll smooth.
4. **First fry:** Deep fry in medium-hot ghee until light golden. Cool slightly.
5. **Stuff:** Make a small cavity in each; fill with reserved chena mixed with a little sugar and camphor. Seal.
6. **Second fry:** Fry again until golden brown. Drain.
7. **Syrup:** Boil 20 g sugar in 40 ml water to 1–2 thread consistency; add a pinch of camphor.
8. **Soak:** Immerse dumplings in warm syrup for 10–15 min. Serve warm.
