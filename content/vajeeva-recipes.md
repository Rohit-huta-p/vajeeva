### S-01 · Barley Roti

**Source:** BK — Dhanyavishesha & Bhakshyavishesha, pp. 13, 51 · ICMR  
**Yield:** 2–3 medium roti

| Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |
|-----------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Barley flour | 80–100 | — | ½ cup | — |
| Water | — | — | — | as needed (warm preferred) |
| Rock salt | — | — | — | a pinch |
| Ghee | — | 4–6 | 1 tsp | — |

**Method**
Take a barley flour in a mixing bowl add pinch of rock salt (if desired) and mix well. Gradually add water and knead into soft and smooth dough (use warm water for kneading to obtain soft rotis) cover the dough and rest it for 15-20min.
Make equal sized ball into dough and dust each ball with a little quantity of flour and roll it into thin, round roti.
Heat a tawa on medium flame and place the rolled roti on it and apply ghee, cook until small bubbles appear then flip the roti and cook the other side by applying the ghee and gently press with spatula for the roti puffs up (put ghee on to the roti, if desired).
Serve hot with curry or dal or chutney.

**Source:** BK — Bhakshyavishesha, p. 53 · ICMR

| Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |

| Sesame oil | — | 10–20 | 2–4 tsp | — |
| Ghee | — | 10–20 | 2–4 tsp | — |

**Method**
Soak the de-husked black gram for 6-8hrs or overnight then grind it in a mixer to obtain a smooth paste.

### S-06 · Baati *(Angarakarakati)*

**Source:** BP 12/33–34 · BK — Bhakshyavishesha, p. 51 · KK 10th Utsava · ICMR  
**Yield:** 2–3 medium baati

| Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |
|-----------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Wheat flour | 80–100 | — | ½ cup | — |
| Ghee | — | — | 3–4 tsp in | dough + extra for finishing |
| Water | — | — | — | as needed |
| Rock salt | — | — | — | a pinch |

**Method**
Take wheat flour in a mixing bowl add pinch of rock salt (if desired) and 3-4tsp. of ghee mix well. Gradually add water and knead into firm dough. Cover the dough and allow it to rest for 15-20 min.
Divide the dough into equal portions and make a medium sized round ball. Place these balls over the coal embers or a wire rack above the charcoal
OR Bake in a preheated oven at 1800C for 25-30 min.
OR use Air fryer by smearing the paper with oil and then place the balls on to the paper for 15 - 20 mins.
OR use preheated pressure cooker for 10 min, with a layer of salt or sand at the bottom of a cooker. Place the wheat balls on a rack and cook on low flame for 30-40min. turning occasionally.
OR use thick bottomed pan over low flame gas stove turning occasionally, once partially cooked, roast them directly over a low flame for a few min. to achieve golden brown colour.
Remove the cooked balls from heat and allow them to cool slightly, then dip it in melted ghee or pour ghee generously over it and serve with dhal.

### S-37 · Stuffed Cottage Cheese Dumplings *(Dugdha Kupika)*

**Source:** BP 12/132–135 · ICMR  
**Yield:** 3–4 dumplings (1 serving)

| Stage | Ingredient | g | ml | Cups | Note |
|-------|-----------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Chena | Cow's milk | — | 500 | — | — |
|  | Lemon juice | — | 10 | — | to curdle |
| Dough | Rice flour | 10 | — | 2 tsp | — |
| Filling | Rock sugar | 20 | — | — | — |
|  | Edible camphor | — | — | — | a pinch |
| Syrup | Sugar | 20 | — | — | + 40 ml water |
| Frying | Ghee | 150 | — | — | — |

**Method**
Prepare Chena (Cottage cheese) :
Boil 500 ml cow’s milk
Add 10 ml lemon juice gradually while stirring until the milk curdles
Strain through a muslin cloth and wash with clean water to remove sourness
Hang for 20-30 min. to drain excess water.
Prepare the Dough :
Transfer the prepared chena to a bowl.
Add 10 gm rice flour
Knead for 8-10 min. until a smooth, crack-free dough is obtained.
Shape the Dumplings :
Divide the dough into 3-4 equal portions (about 25-30gm each)
Roll into smooth balls.
First frying :
Heat ghee over medium flame
Deep-fry the balls until light golden brown
Remove and allow them to cool slightly
Stuffing :
Make a small cavity in the centre of each fried ball.
Fill with a small amount of the reserved chena
Seal the opening carefully using a little dough
Second frying :
Fry the filled dumplings again until golden brown and fully cooked
Remove and drain excess ghee
Sugar syrup :
Dissolve 20 gm sugar in 40 ml water
Boil until a 1-2 thread consistency is reached
Add a tiny pinch of edible camphor
Final step :
Immerse the fried dumplings in the warm sugar syrup for 10-15 min. and serve warm.
