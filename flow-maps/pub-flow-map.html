8 · Safety, edge & offline P42–P46

Cross-cutting states — these overlay every screen above rather than forming a path.

P42 · SAFETYFlag = diabetes → unsafe recipes flagged/filtered everywhere; Detail shows strong warning.
P43 · OFFLINESaved + Cook Mode fully work offline; Browse shows offline state.
P44 · IMAGE MISSINGBranded illustration placeholder — never a broken image.
P45 · PREP-AHEAD"Start tonight" nudge → Detail overnight step.
P46 · NOTIFICATIONTimer complete — discreet wording, no health terms → deep-link back into recipe.
